# Preview: build plan

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-30-photo-cleanup-design.md`, section "Preview".

The user wants to see a photo or play a video at full size before deciding to delete it.
Today a grid tile only toggles its decision, and only swipe-deck videos can play.

## Decision

A touch and hold on a tile opens a full-screen viewer over the grid's own items, in grid order.
A tap keeps its current meaning on every screen.
In the swipe deck, a tap on any card opens the same viewer.

| Option | Entry | Why not / why |
|---|---|---|
| Full-screen viewer, chosen | Touch and hold a tile, tap a deck card | Matches the Photos app, keeps tap as the toggle, lets the user page through neighbours and play videos with sound. |
| Context-menu peek | Touch and hold | No paging, no video controls, no decision button. |
| Expand button on each tile | Tap a small button | Tiles are about 90 pt and already hold a mark, a badge and two labels. |

## Data shape

- The viewer gets the grid's frozen `[AssetInfo]` order, the shown `AssetID?` (nil means closed), and one `PreviewChoice` per asset.
- `PreviewChoice` is plain data: an optional status (text and tint) and a list of actions (title, tint, closure).
  Each screen builds it from the state it already owns, so the viewer holds no decision logic.

| Screen | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots, Large videos | "Selected to delete" (red) or "Not selected" | "Don't delete" or "Select to delete", the same as a tile tap |
| Free up | "Will be deleted" (red) or "Keeping" (green) | "Keep" or "Delete after all", the same as a tile tap |
| Apply, either section | "Will be kept" (green), "Will be deleted" (red) or "Taken back" | "Take back" or "Restore", the same as a tile tap |
| Swipe deck | none | "Delete" and "Keep", the same as the deck's buttons, then the viewer closes |

## Steps

1. **Viewer.**
   A full-screen view with lazy horizontal paging, so it stays smooth over a Free up grid of tens of thousands of items.
   Photos load at screen size through `PhotoLibrary.thumbnail`.
   Videos play with system controls, and only the page on screen plays.
   A bottom bar shows date, size, video length, position ("12 of 240"), the status and the actions.
   A Close button dismisses it.
   Check: build passes with zero warnings.
2. **Grids.**
   Free up, Screenshots, Large videos and Apply open the viewer on touch and hold, and expose a "Preview" accessibility action.
   Closing scrolls the grid to the last item shown.
   Hint copy names the gesture.
   Check: build passes.
3. **Swipe deck.**
   A tap on any card opens the viewer with Keep and Delete.
   `VideoPlayerSheet` is deleted, because the viewer replaces it.
   Check: build passes.
4. **UI test.**
   On the Apply screen, touch and hold a queued delete, take it back in the viewer, close, and check the tile and the Delete count.
   Check: the full `xcodebuild test` run passes on the iOS 26.4 simulator.
5. **Look at it.**
   Seed one short video, then screenshot the viewer on a photo and on a playing video.
6. **Review.**
   A fresh reviewer reads the diff, focused on the decision each action writes and on video playback across pages.

## Not yet specified

- Pinch to zoom on photos.

## Out of scope

- Live Photo playback.
- Editing or sharing from the viewer.
