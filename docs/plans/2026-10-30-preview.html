Approve: touch and hold a photo to see it full screen, built in six checked steps

You see a photo or play a video at full size before you decide, and change its choice from the same screen.

What changes

+
A full-screen viewer. Swipe left or right through the grid's own items. Videos play with sound, and only the one on screen plays. A bar shows the date, size, position, the choice and a button that changes it.
+
Free up space, Screenshots, Large videos and Apply open the viewer on touch and hold. Closing it scrolls the grid to the last item you saw.
+
In the swipe deck, a tap on any card opens the viewer with Keep and Delete.
−
The old video player in the swipe deck, because the viewer replaces it. VideoPlayerSheet
+
A UI test on the iOS 26.4 simulator. It takes back a queued delete from the viewer on the Apply screen and checks the tile and the Delete count.
=
A tap on a tile still flips its choice on every screen.

Risk → answer

The viewer's button writes the wrong choice.
Each screen builds the viewer's buttons from its own state, the same as a tile tap. The UI test and a fresh reviewer check the choice each button writes. PreviewChoice
Paging stutters on a big Free up space grid.
The viewer loads pages lazily, so it stays smooth over tens of thousands of items.
A video keeps playing after you swipe away.
Only the page on screen plays. The reviewer checks playback across pages.

For you to decide

Reply "approved" or say what to change.

Already decided

  • A full-screen viewer, opened by touch and hold on a tile or a tap on a swipe card. It matches the Photos app, keeps tap as the toggle, and lets you page through neighbours and play videos with sound.
  • No context-menu peek. It has no paging, no video controls and no choice button.
  • No expand button on each tile. Tiles are about 90 pt and already hold a mark, a badge and two labels.
  • Each screen gives the viewer its status and buttons, so the viewer holds no decision logic.

Left for later: nothing; pinch to zoom has its own plan, docs/plans/2026-11-12-photo-zoom.md.

Not in this change: Live Photo playback; editing or sharing from the viewer.
