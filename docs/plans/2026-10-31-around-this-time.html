Risk → answer

A batch write puts photos in the wrong album, or leaves a delete queued.
A fresh reviewer checks every album write and the delete queue rule. Unit tests pin the rule for many photos.
Changing the album calls breaks the album features that work today.
Step 1 updates the existing album tests, and the existing album UI tests run in step 7.
The pinch fights the swipe.
A UI test pinches the card, and the swipe tests still run. If the two fight, the pinch goes and the button stays.
Five buttons do not fit on a small iPhone.
Step 6 checks the deck on a 375 pt wide simulator.
