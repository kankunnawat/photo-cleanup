Approve: pinch or double-tap a photo in the viewer to zoom in on a sharper copy

You check a photo's detail at up to 4x before you keep or delete it, and a swipe at the zoomed edge still moves to the next photo.

What changes

+
Pinch a photo in the viewer to zoom in, between 2x and 4x depending on how many pixels it has. A zoom that ends just above fit snaps back to fit.
+
Double-tap zooms in on the point you tapped. Double-tap again to return to fit.
+
A drag pans a zoomed photo. A swipe at its edge moves to the next photo, as in Photos. Leaving a photo returns it to fit.
+
When a zoom settles, the viewer loads a sharper copy, no bigger than the original, without moving the photo. ZoomablePhoto ZoomMath
+
Unit tests for the zoom rules. UI tests for pinch, double-tap, pan, paging while zoomed, and Close.
=
Videos, the swipe deck card and grid tiles do not zoom. A tap on a tile still flips its choice.

Risk → answer

You get stuck on a zoomed photo.
A swipe at the zoomed edge moves on, as the prototype showed. A zoom that ends below 1.05x returns exactly to fit.
The sharper copy makes the photo jump.
The photo's frame comes from its pixel shape, not from the loaded image. In the prototype, a copy swapped in mid-zoom left the photo where it was.
A big photo uses too much memory.
The sharper copy is sized to the zoom on screen, capped at the photo's own size, and dropped when you leave the photo.
The simulator can't show sharpness.
Its seed photos are 1200 x 1600. A unit test checks the size the viewer asks for, and you check a real photo on your phone.

For you to decide

  1. How far should zoom go?I recommend up to 4x, and never less than 2x: a big photo zooms until one of its pixels fills two screen pixels, and a small or blurry photo still zooms enough to show the blur.

Reply "approved" to take the recommendation, or say what to change.

Already decided

  • UIKit's zooming view on each photo page, chosen over SwiftUI's pinch gesture. Only it lets a swipe at the zoomed edge move on, and its UI tests can read where the photo is.
  • Double-tap to zoom, as in Photos.
  • Videos do not zoom.

Left for later: whether 4x is enough to judge sharpness on 48 MP photos.

Not in this change: zooming videos, the swipe deck card or grid tiles; Live Photo playback.
