The Keep album is a normal Photos album, so it survives an app reinstall and you can edit it in Photos. 3 · How it runs

Nothing is deleted until you press Apply

  1. 1Swipe a month: right keeps, left deletes, Undo takes back a swipe made in this deck and never touches a choice made elsewhere. Free up space, Screenshots and Large videos queue deletes too, never for a protected photo; in Free up space, tap a photo to keep it and tap again to clear it. Touch and hold a photo, or tap a swipe card, to see it full screen with a button that changes its choice.
  2. 2Each choice waits as a pending decision, saved on the phone half a second after your last choice and again when you leave the app, so closing it loses nothing. On the Apply screen, tap a photo to switch it between keep and delete in place. Take back all clears a whole section, and Restore all puts it back.
  3. 3Apply adds the keepers to the Keep album first, and drops any queued delete that became protected since.
  4. 4Then one delete for the rest. iOS shows its own confirmation; Cancel leaves the deletes queued.
  5. 5Deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space until you empty it there.

Risk → answer

A photo you want to keep gets deleted.
Bulk modes never queue a protected photo, and Apply checks protection again right before the delete. Every delete goes through iOS's own confirmation.
iCloud Photos is on, so a delete here also deletes from iCloud and your other devices.
A one-time notice at first launch says so before you can delete anything.
Deleted photos still use space for 30 days in Recently Deleted.
After each delete the app says so plainly and points you to Recently Deleted. Freed space reads "up to", because Optimize iPhone Storage keeps only small copies of some photos.
The app cannot see whether your backup is complete.
Free up space asks you to confirm the backup is complete. Backup services do not show an app what they hold (the Google Photos API shows only media the calling app created), so this stays your check.
Free Personal Team signing expires after 7 days.
You reinstall from Xcode once a week. The Keep album lives in Photos, so it survives a reinstall. A paid developer account removes the expiry.

Already decided

  • A native SwiftUI app for iOS 18 and later, so it runs on your iPhone and on the iOS 18.6 simulator.
  • The Keep album in Photos is the lasting keep marker, not app storage.
  • All your albums are protected by default; the Keep album and Favorites are always protected.
  • Apply sends every delete in one batch, which iOS confirms, and the photos go to Recently Deleted.
  • Undo works per swipe deck, and tiles in Free up space and Apply change in place, so nothing shifts under your finger. A tile leaves only when you close the viewer after adding its photo to a protected album.
  • Touch and hold a tile, or tap a swipe card, to see it full screen. A tap on a tile still flips its choice.
  • Signed with your free Personal Team; you reinstall weekly.
  • Similar-photo detection waits for phase 2.

Left for later: how Shuffle weights old versus recent photos; whether the size cache refreshes when Photos edits a photo in place; pinch to zoom on photos.

Not in this change: similar and duplicate photo detection (phase 2); contacts, calendar and email cleanup; emptying Recently Deleted (no public API); reading backup status from a backup service; App Store release, iPad layout, widgets; Live Photo playback.
