Approve: zoom out from a card to the photos around its date, and add many to an album at once

The swipe deck gains an "Around this time" button. It opens a grid of every photo and video from 3 days before to 3 days after the card, where you pick the ones that belong together and add them, keep them or delete them in one step.

3 · How it runs

Put a workout in an album from the deck

  1. 1On a gym photo, tap Around this time. The grid opens on Apr 26 to May 2, scrolled to that photo, which is selected.
  2. 2Tap the other workout photos to select them. Touch and hold one to check it full screen.
  3. 3Tap Add 4 to album and pick Gym. All four go into Gym in one Photos change, and any pending delete on them is cleared.
  4. 4Gym is protected, so each of the four shows a lock. Keep and Delete no longer count them.
  5. 5Go back. The four cards are gone from the deck, and the photos you did not pick are still there to swipe.

Names: PhotoLibrary.add(_:toAlbum:) now takes many photos, AppModel.queueKeep

Risk → answer

One tap now changes many photos' albums.
The picker title says how many ("Add 4 to album"), a dash shows a partial album, and a second tap takes them all out again. Album changes never delete a photo.
Delete in the grid removes photos you did not mean to.
Delete only queues them. Nothing is deleted until you confirm on the Apply screen, where you can take any of them back.
Delete or Keep picks up a protected photo.
Both count only unprotected photos, and protected tiles show a lock.
The grid's decisions empty the deck, and the deck jumps to Apply under the grid.
The deck jumps only while it is on screen. Going back shows "All done here".
The pinch fights the swipe.
A UI test pinches the card and the swipe tests still run. If they fight, the pinch goes and the button stays.
A busy day holds hundreds of photos.
The grid loads thumbnails only as they scroll into view, as the other grids do.

For you to decide

  1. Should the grid also keep or delete the photos you pick?I recommend yes. A run of near-identical shots goes to the delete queue in one tap, and a set you want kept without an album goes to Keep in one tap. Deletes still wait for the Apply screen.
  2. Should the 7 days cross into the next or previous month?I recommend yes. A card from April 29 then also shows May 1 and May 2, so a workout week or a trip that spans a month end shows in one grid.

Reply "approved" to take the recommendation, or say what to change.

Already decided

  • The grid opens from a new round button in the deck and from pinching the card closed.
  • The window is the card's day and 3 days each side, in the phone's time zone.
  • The grid shows photos and videos, decided and protected ones too, whatever the deck's filter is.
  • Oldest first under one header per day, as in Photos, opened on the card's photo.
  • The album picker takes many photos; with one photo it works as it does today.
  • Adding to an album keeps the selection, so you can add the same photos to a second album.

Left for later: whether 7 days is the right width once you have used it on real days.

Not in this change: opening the grid from other screens; selecting a whole day or dragging to select; sharing many photos at once; finding workouts or similar photos automatically; changing the window width in the app.
