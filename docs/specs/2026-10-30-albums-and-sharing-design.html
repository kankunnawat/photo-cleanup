A tap on an album adds the photo in Photos at once, and a second tap removes it. There is no Apply step for albums, because they never delete a photo. Every album is protected unless you switched it off in Settings, so the photo leaves Free up space, Screenshots, Large videos and the deck. Share copies the photo to a file first, then opens the iPhone share sheet with it.

Names: PhotoLibrary, AppModel, PHAssetCollectionChangeRequest

3 · How it runs

Add to an album from the deck

  1. 1Tap Album under the top card. The album picker opens as a sheet over the deck.
  2. 2Pick an album, or tap New album and type a name. The photo goes into that album in Photos at once, and the row shows a check mark.
  3. 3The album is protected, so the photo joins the protected set. Free up space, Screenshots and Large videos stop offering it for deletion.
  4. 4A pending delete on the photo is cleared, so the Apply screen never counts it. A pending keep stays.
  5. 5Tap Done. The card leaves the deck and the next card comes up.

Names: PhotoLibrary.add(_:toAlbum:), PendingDecisions, ProtectedSet

Risk → answer

An album change has no Apply step to back out of.
A second tap on the row takes the photo out again, and the check mark shows where it is. Adding or removing never deletes a photo.
The Apply screen counts a photo that Apply would skip.
Adding to a protected album clears that photo's pending delete at once.
After an add from the viewer, a Delete, Keep or Restore tap queues the protected photo again, and Apply or Free up space still counts it.
The viewer shows "Protected" with no buttons for a protected photo. When the viewer closes, Apply and Free up space drop its tile.
You add a photo to an album you switched off in Settings and expect it to be safe.
The row says "Not protected" before you tap.
Taking a photo out of an album puts it up for deletion.
Removing never queues a delete. You choose again in Free up space or the deck.
The original is only in iCloud, so sharing stalls.
The Share button shows a spinner and reads "Cancel" while the file downloads. A tap cancels the download.
Exported files take space on the phone.
They go to the app's temporary folder. They are removed when the share sheet closes, and the app clears the folder at launch.

For you to decide

  1. Should the swipe deck get its own Album button?I recommend adding it as a fourth round button between Undo and Keep. Putting a photo in an album while you swipe then takes one tap and a pick, and you do not open the viewer first.
  2. Should adding a photo to an album clear its pending delete?I recommend yes. A photo added to a protected album leaves the delete queue at once, so the Apply screen never counts a photo that Apply would skip.

Already decided

  • Album changes save to Photos at once, with no Apply step, because they never delete a photo.
  • The Keep album is not in the picker, because Keep has its own button everywhere.
  • Shared and smart albums are not listed, because iOS does not let an app add to them.
  • Sharing sends the edited version if the photo or video has edits, else the original. A photo goes as stored, and a video goes as a movie file.
  • Sharing copies the file before the share sheet opens, so the copy can be deleted afterwards and an iCloud download can be cancelled.
  • A Live Photo shares as its still image.

Left for later: whether the Recent list should also learn from albums you use in Photos.

Not in this change: browsing an album's photos in the app; selecting many photos in a grid to add or share at once; renaming, deleting or nesting albums; sharing Live Photos as live.
